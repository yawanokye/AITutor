SAMPLE COURSE NOTE

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants use light energy to convert carbon dioxide and water into glucose. Oxygen is released as a by-product. Chlorophyll in the chloroplast absorbs light energy. The simplified word equation is carbon dioxide plus water produces glucose plus oxygen, in the presence of light and chlorophyll.
